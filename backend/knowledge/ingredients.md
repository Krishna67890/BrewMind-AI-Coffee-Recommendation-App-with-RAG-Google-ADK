# BrewMind Ingredients Guide

## Coffee Beans
- **Midnight Roast**: 100% Arabica beans sourced from Ethiopia and Brazil. Dark roasted for notes of dark chocolate and smoke.
- **House Blend**: A medium roast blend with citrus and nutty undertones.

## Milk Options
- **Whole Milk**: Standard full-fat dairy milk.
- **Oat Milk**: Barista-grade creamy oat milk (Vegan).
- **Almond Milk**: Unsweetened roasted almond milk (Vegan, Contains Nuts).
- **Soy Milk**: High-protein soy milk (Vegan, Contains Soy).

## Sweeteners & Syrups
- **Vanilla Bean Syrup**: Made with real Madagascar vanilla beans.
- **Caramel Sauce**: Rich buttery caramel (Contains Dairy).
- **Dark Chocolate Ganache**: 70% cocoa (Contains Dairy).
- **Honey**: Organic wildflower honey.
- **Stevia**: Natural calorie-free sweetener.
