# BrewMind Coffee Guide

## The Art of Extraction
Our espresso is extracted at exactly 9 bars of pressure at 93°C (200°F). We use a 1:2 brewing ratio for our signature Midnight Roast.

## Understanding Caffeine
- **Espresso Shot**: ~75mg caffeine.
- **Nitro Cold Brew**: ~215mg per 12oz (Slow-steeped extraction increases caffeine content).
- **Matcha**: ~70mg caffeine (Provides a more sustained release due to L-theanine).
- **Decaf**: We use the Swiss Water Process to remove 99.9% of caffeine.

## Temperature Guide
- **Hot Coffee**: Served at 65°C-70°C to ensure optimal flavor without scalding the milk.
- **Cold Brew**: Stealthed at 4°C for 18 hours.
- **Iced Drinks**: Served over triple-filtered ice.

## Milk Steaming
- **Latte**: 1cm of microfoam.
- **Cappuccino**: 2-3cm of airy foam.
- **Flat White**: 0.5cm of extremely fine microfoam.
