# BrewMind AI - Frequently Asked Questions

## General
**Q: What is BrewMind AI?**
A: BrewMind AI is your personal coffee concierge. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to understand your mood and preferences to recommend the perfect drink from our menu.

**Q: Where are you located?**
A: We have three fictional locations in Downtown, Mid-City, and the Tech Park.

## Ordering & Pickup
**Q: Can I order online?**
A: Yes, you can use our Smart Order builder to customize your drink and place an order for pickup.

**Q: How long does an order take?**
A: Most drinks are ready within 5-7 minutes of placing the order.

## Dietary & Health
**Q: Do you have sugar-free options?**
A: Yes, our Nitro Silk Cold Brew and Midnight Espresso are naturally sugar-free. We also offer Stevia as a sweetener for any drink.

**Q: Are your milk alternatives unsweetened?**
A: Our Almond milk is unsweetened. Our Oat and Soy milks contain minimal natural sugars but no added cane sugar.

## Technology
**Q: How does the AI recommendation work?**
A: We use a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. The AI searches our specific product database and ingredient guides to find the best match for your requested caffeine level, sweetness, and mood.
