# Allergen Information

At BrewMind AI, we take food safety seriously. Please review the following allergen information:

## Common Allergens in our Products:
- **Dairy**: Present in all "Velvet" series, "Cappuccino", "Mocha", "Macchiato", "Latte" (unless milk alternative is chosen), "Cheesecake", "Tart", "Brownie", "Croissant", "Feta Wrap".
- **Gluten**: Present in "Truffle Mushroom Toast", "Avocado Sourdough", "Butter Croissant", "Spinach Feta Wrap", "Espresso Brownie", "New York Cheesecake", "Lemon Drizzle Cake", "Dark Chocolate Tart", "Blueberry Muffin", "Almond Biscotti".
- **Eggs**: Present in "Spinach Feta Wrap", "Espresso Brownie", "Lemon Drizzle Cake", "Blueberry Muffin", "Pistachio Macaron".
- **Nuts**: Present in "Hazelnut Frappe" (Hazelnuts), "Almond Biscotti" (Almonds), "Pistachio Macaron" (Pistachios).
- **Soy**: Present in Soy Milk option.

## Cross-Contamination Warning:
While we offer dairy-free and gluten-free options, our kitchen handles gluten, dairy, eggs, and nuts. We cannot guarantee 100% absence of cross-contamination.
